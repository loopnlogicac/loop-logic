Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Loop & Logic (“we”) runs this learning website for students in Bangladesh. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. Contact: loopnlogic.ac@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Without an account: a random learner ID stored in a cookie, so your progress (videos watched, quiz answers, lessons read, practice attempts) can be saved. It is not linked to your name.
  • If you sign in with Google or an email link: your email address, and your name and profile picture if Google provides them. We use them only to identify your account and show who is signed in.
  • Questions you ask the AI helper, and the text you select when you ask. They are sent to our AI providers to produce an answer.
  • Waitlist: if you join the Pro waitlist, the email you enter.
  • Classes: if you join an instructor’s class with a class code, the class you joined.
  • Code you run: when you press Run on code you changed or wrote, the code and the input you typed are sent to a code sandbox to run. We do not keep a copy linked to you; identical programs may be kept briefly so they run faster the second time.
  • Paid courses: if you buy a course, the bKash or Nagad transaction ID, the last 4 digits of the number you paid from, the amount and any coupon code. We never ask for your PIN, OTP or full account number.
  • Premium videos: to stop one account being shared, we keep a random ID for each device you watch on and its type (for example "Chrome on Android"), up to 2 devices. While you watch a premium video, your email and a short account code move across the player, so a recording can be traced. Ask us to reset your devices if you change phone or computer.

Why we use it

  • To save your learning progress and show it on your dashboard and on every device you sign in on.
  • To adapt lessons and practice to what you have already learned.
  • To answer your questions with the AI helper.
  • To show instructors anonymous, combined statistics about their courses. Groups smaller than a minimum size are never shown.
  • If you join a class, to show that class’s instructor your progress in it: topics opened, quiz scores, labs run and assignments done. Your questions to the AI are never shown to instructors. You can leave a class at any time from your dashboard.
  • To check a payment and unlock the course you bought.
  • To email you when Pro opens, only if you joined the waitlist.

We do not sell your data, show third-party advertising, or use your data to train AI models. Some pages show promotions for our own courses; we only count how often they are seen and clicked, without tracking you.

Who processes it

  • Supabase (database and sign-in), servers in Singapore.
  • Render (website hosting).
  • Google (sign-in with Google, and the Gemini AI model) and Groq (backup AI model). Your question and the relevant lesson text are sent to them to produce an answer.
  • Judge0 (code sandbox, through RapidAPI) runs the code you change or write. Only the code and its input are sent, not your name or email. Unchanged examples are answered by our own server.
  • YouTube plays the course videos (privacy-enhanced mode, youtube-nocookie.com).

Your choices

  • You can learn without signing in.
  • On your profile page you can download everything we store about you, or delete your reading history.
  • To delete your account completely, email us from the address you signed in with and we will do it within 14 days.

Children

Parts of the site are written for young learners. If you are under 13, please use the site with a parent or teacher, and do not sign in without their permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, show a notice on the site.
