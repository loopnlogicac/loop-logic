How it works

Read, ask without prompting, prove it

Students don’t need to know how to talk to an AI. They read, tap what confuses them, and the platform does the rest — from sources a teacher approved.

Read a lesson that fits you

Wording, term help and examples adjust to how you read. Facts, formulas and numbers never change — and you can always see the original.

Select a line, get a short answer

Explain, search trusted sources or take a quick quiz. Answers cite the passage they came from; if the AI can’t back it up, you get the reviewed text instead.

Prove it on a sealed check

Practise judging an AI coworker’s advice, then solve a fresh task with no help. You get an evidence receipt showing what you did alone and what needed help.
